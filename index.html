Why Cold Plunge

BUILT FOR
RECOVERY

⚡
Muscle Recovery
Cold immersion constricts blood vessels, reducing inflammation and micro-tears in muscle tissue. When you warm up, blood rushes back in — flushing metabolic waste and delivering nutrients faster.
↓ 66% reduction in delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS)
🧠
Mental Sharpness
A 10-minute cold plunge releases norepinephrine by up to 300%. This neurochemical sharply improves focus, vigilance, and mood — lasting for hours after your session.
↑ 300% norepinephrine increase (Søberg, 2021)
🔥
Metabolism Boost
Shivering and rewarming activate brown adipose tissue (BAT), which burns calories to generate heat. Regular cold exposure increases metabolic rate and insulin sensitivity over time.
↑ Brown fat activation linked to long-term metabolic health
😴
Deeper Sleep
Cold exposure in the afternoon accelerates the natural drop in core body temperature that signals your brain to sleep — resulting in faster sleep onset and more time in deep restorative stages.
↑ Improved sleep efficiency reported in 74% of users
🛡️
Immune Resilience
Studies from the Czech Republic and Netherlands show regular cold water swimmers have significantly higher white blood cell counts and lower rates of respiratory illness compared to control groups.
↑ 40% fewer sick days vs. control group (Kox et al., 2014)
⬆️
Dopamine & Mood
Cold plunges produce a sustained 250–270% increase in dopamine — unlike the fleeting spike from sugar or social media, this elevation lasts 2–4 hours with no crash afterward.
↑ 250–270% dopamine baseline increase (Šrámek et al.)

The Process

YOUR SESSION
IN 4 STEPS

01
Arrive & Prepare
Check in, change, and take 3 minutes of slow, controlled breathing to prepare your nervous system.
02
Immerse
Step into 50–59°F water. Our staff guides your breathing through the initial cold shock response.
Target: 50–59°F / 10–15°C
03
Hold the Line
Stay immersed for 2–10 minutes. Focus on calm nasal breathing. This is where the adaptation happens.
Protocol: 2–10 minutes
04
Rewarm & Reflect
Exit and allow your body to rewarm naturally. Dopamine and norepinephrine peak over the next 30 minutes.

The Science

Cold water immersion triggers a cascade of physiological adaptations — from reduced inflammatory markers to optimized neurotransmitter output. These aren't placebo effects. They're documented, peer-reviewed responses.
